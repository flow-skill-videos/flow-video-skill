# Example 001 — Morning Greens

First end-to-end workflow used to validate the skill.

## Character
Josh — recurring mature male wellness-creator archetype with a saved voice.

## Format
Vertical 9:16 smartphone UGC.

## Structure

1. Visual hook — 6s
2. Misconception/reframe — 8s
3. Simple-routine transition — 8s
4. Product demonstration — 6s
5. Anti-hype credibility — 8s
6. Soft CTA — 6s

Total planned duration: 42 seconds.

## Dialogue

### Scene 1 — 6s
"If your stomach looks flat in the morning but puffy by dinner, watch this."

### Scene 2 — 8s
"A lot of people blame body fat, but sometimes the change is just food volume, gas, and water."

### Scene 3 — 8s
"That is why I keep my morning routine simple and consistent instead of chasing random hacks."

### Scene 4 — 6s
"I mix one scoop of Morning Greens with cold water and drink it with breakfast."

### Scene 5 — 8s
"It is not magic. I just like having one easy routine I can actually stick to."

### Scene 6 — 6s
"If you want to try the same routine, check out Morning Greens."

## Production lessons

- Character first, then voice, then product reference, then scenes.
- Alternate still-image generation and video animation one scene at a time in guided mode.
- Reuse the previous scene as continuity reference when possible.
- Keep dialogue embedded directly in the video prompt.
- Use hard cuts during assembly.
- Add captions in post rather than baking them into generated clips.

This example is a production reference, not a requirement that future videos use the same product, character, health topic, scene count or kitchen setting.
