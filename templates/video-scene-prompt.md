# Video Scene Prompt Template

Use this template to produce copy-paste-ready prompts for Gemini Omni Flash.

```text
Use the provided [SCENE NAME] image as the strict visual starting reference and use [CHARACTER] as the exact saved character identity.

DURATION:
Exactly [4|6|8|10] seconds.

FORMAT:
Vertical 9:16.

STYLE:
[STYLE PROFILE]

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:
Keep [CHARACTER] exactly identical to the saved character and previous scenes.
Preserve face, apparent age, skin tone, eyes, hairstyle, hair color, facial hair, body proportions, wardrobe and overall identity.
Do not redesign, beautify or age-shift the character.

ENVIRONMENT CONTINUITY:
Keep the exact environment from the supplied image and prior scene.
Maintain architecture, props, lighting direction, daylight/time-of-day and color temperature.

PRODUCT / PROP CONSISTENCY:
[INSERT PRODUCT OR PROP RULES]

ACTION:
[DESCRIBE EXACT PHYSICAL ACTIONS IN ORDER]

FACIAL PERFORMANCE:
[DESCRIBE EXPRESSION, EYE CONTACT, NODS, SMILES, BLINKING]

CAMERA:
One continuous shot unless the reference requires otherwise.
[CAMERA POSITION / MOVEMENT]
Avoid unnecessary cinematic movement.

VOICE:
Use [VOICE IDENTITY] exactly.
Maintain vocal age, accent, pitch, rasp/breathiness, cadence, energy and personality.
Tone for this scene: [TONE].

DIALOGUE:
[CHARACTER] must say EXACTLY:

"[FULL SPOKEN LINE]"

DIALOGUE RULES:
Finish the complete line naturally within the selected duration.
Do not paraphrase.
Do not add words.
Do not remove words.
Do not repeat words.
Only [CHARACTER] speaks.
Use accurate natural lip synchronization.

AUDIO:
Clean close smartphone-style voice.
[AMBIENCE / OBJECT SOUNDS]
No background music unless specifically requested.

IMPORTANT:
No generated subtitles.
No captions.
No text overlays.
No watermarks.
No extra people unless explicitly required.
[OTHER NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS]
```
